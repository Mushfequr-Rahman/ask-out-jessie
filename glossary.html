Design notes.

Mining ship / Small

Ore carrier

The original compact mining workhorse. A large ore hold, loading crane, and rugged hull keep Magneton moving from the mines to the cities.

Mining ship / Small

Mole

A proposed squat mining variant with a detachable ore cassette, articulated loading arms, and a forward drill. Its short body is designed for work close to a deposit.

Open full-size artwork ↗
Schooner / Small

City schooner

A slender commuter Zeppelin for travel between districts of a city or settlements within a duchy. Responsive magnetic motors favor mobility over cargo capacity.

Open full-size artwork ↗
Schooner / Small

Needle

A proposed light commuter variant with a teardrop hull, compact cockpit, and articulated engine outriggers. A smaller cabin trades carrying space for agility.

Open full-size artwork ↗
Schooner / Small

Kestrel

An open-deck commuter variant. A swept lower hull carries a glass cabin and small boarding deck, with magnetic rings mounted around its edges.

Open full-size artwork ↗
Trading ship / Large

Passenger trader

A large merchant vessel with freight holds, loading equipment, and passenger decks. Its capacity connects cities and duchies, but its bulk makes navigation difficult.

Open full-size artwork ↗
Trading ship / Large

Caravan

A proposed twin-hull trader. Cargo bridges connect two parallel Zeppelin hulls, with freight between them and passenger space above. The broad form favors carrying capacity.

Open full-size artwork ↗
Trading ship / Large

Terrace

A proposed broad-deck trader with passenger terraces above freight holds, a panoramic forward saloon, and a raised aft bridge. Its broad, deck-led silhouette favors carrying space over agility.

Man-of-war / Capital

Ducal man-of-war

An armored capital ship with broadside gun decks and massive magnetic engines. These are the most expensive and rarest vessels; many duchies possess no more than three capital ships in total.

Open full-size artwork ↗
Man-of-war / Capital

Bastion

A proposed broad, three-keel fortress variant with side hulls, a raised command citadel, and recessed engines. It belongs to the same rare capital class, not a separate allowance of ships.

A few words from this world.

Magneton
The metal formed from nuclear materials, used in the magnetic technology that powers cities and ships.
Duchy
A grouping of floating cities within the new feudal order.
Magnetic motor
An engine using opposing polarities to power transport and city systems.
Baron
A member of the ruling class whose power grew from Magneton extraction, ships, and trade.
