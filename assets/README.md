# Magneton panel artwork

Generated illustrations for this original fictional setting. The artwork uses dark teal, ochre, orange, and mint tones in an inked graphic novel style. Lettering is separate HTML, not baked into the images.

- `fracture.png`: shattered landmasses and magnetic fields.
- `survivors.png`: humanity surviving and adapting on floating islands.
- `ore.png`: discovery and extraction of Magneton.
- `engine.png`: paired polarities inside a magnetic motor.
- `city.png`: a floating city supported by Magneton technology.
- `mining-ship.png`: a small Zeppelin-style ore carrier with a large hold.
- `schooner.png`: a small, agile commuter Zeppelin.
- `trading-ship.png`: a large freight and passenger Zeppelin.
- `man-of-war.png`: a rare, expensive armored capital Zeppelin.

Selected images appear in more than one panel as establishing views or details. They are concept depictions of the world; character appearances and machine designs are not yet fixed story canon.

The four ship designs follow the user-provided rigid metal Zeppelin reference. Each ship can be opened at full resolution from the fleet gallery.
