THE MATERIALS OF THE FRACTURED WORLD

Three metals.
One new world.

From the hull of a ship to the edge of a sword.

Magneton is a family of metals with distinct properties. Type A builds the fleet. Type B powers precision machinery. The recently discovered Type C opens a new chapter in armour and weapons.

The three types.

ATHE MOST COMMON

Type A

The metal of the hull.

A bronze-like metal prized for its durability. It is the most common form of Magneton and is used to build ship hulls.

CRECENTLY DISCOVERED

Type C

Silver that changes the balance of power.

The most malleable type of Magneton, and the most difficult to find. This recently discovered silver metal is impenetrable and impervious to weapons. It can drift along the magnetic fields; Rovan alone knows how to craft it into the armour and swords that give his honour guard this advantage.

Rovan’s five.

The Type C seized aboard the Auric Reach Caravan yields exactly five armour-and-sword sets. Rovan’s secret crafting knowledge lets the honour guard exploit magnetic-field drift to cross between ships. Their armour cannot be penetrated by weapons. He has no material for a sixth set.
